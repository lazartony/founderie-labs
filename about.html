About Founderie Labs

A foundry for software: you bring the idea, we forge the product. Founded by engineers who were tired of watching businesses get sold bad technology.

Why “Founderie”

A foundry takes raw material and, with heat, pressure and skill, turns it into something that lasts. That is how we think about software: your idea and your business knowledge are the raw material; engineering discipline is the heat and pressure; the result should still be running, and earning, years from now.

What we believe

  • Engineering is a craft, and a responsibility. Software that is slow, insecure or unmaintainable is a liability we refuse to ship.
  • Non-technical people deserve technical honesty. Jargon is usually a way to avoid accountability. We explain, in plain language, until you can make the decision yourself.
  • Incentives should be aligned. That is why we offer royalty and equity models: we are willing to be paid when you succeed.
  • Ownership belongs to the client. Your code, your accounts, your data. Always.
  • Speed matters. Fast software, fast answers, fast decisions. Every week ends with something shipped.

What we do

Founderie Labs is three things in one company. A product studio: we design, build and launch our own software products; see what we’re building. A services team: we build websites, custom software and mobile apps for clients, for cash, royalty or equity. A consultancy: fractional CTO work, technology audits, IT training and dedicated engineers. The products keep us honest: we ship client work to the same standard we hold our own.

Who we are

Founderie Labs LLP is a limited liability partnership registered in India, founded by experienced engineers who have built and run production systems across web, mobile, cloud and data. We work remotely with clients in India, North America, the UK and Europe, the Middle East, Singapore and Australia. We are deliberately small: every project is led by a partner.

We are a young company and we don’t yet publish client case studies. We think you should judge us by the products we ship ourselves, by a conversation, and by the terms we are willing to put in writing (fixed scope and price, weekly demos, full ownership and a defect warranty) rather than by a wall of logos.
