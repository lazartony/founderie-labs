IT Consulting

Independent, plain-English technology advice for business owners who need to make the right decision before spending money.

Fractional CTO

Senior technology leadership on a part-time retainer. Strategy, hiring, vendor management and architecture, without a full-time salary.

Technology & security audit

We inspect your current systems, code, hosting and security and give you a prioritised, costed report you can act on, or hand to another vendor.

Digital transformation roadmap

Where to automate, what to build, what to buy, in what order: a 12–24 month plan tied to business outcomes, not buzzwords.

Vendor selection & oversight

Cloud & cost review

Most companies overpay for hosting and licences by 30% or more. We find it, fix it, and set up controls.

AI readiness

Which parts of your business can AI actually help with today, what data you need, and what to ignore. Honest, hype-free.

Why independent advice matters

We don’t sell hardware, licences or hours you don’t need.

When a vendor advises you, they are selling. When we advise you, our only product is the recommendation, and we will happily tell you when the right answer is a US$20/month off-the-shelf tool rather than custom software.

Engagements typically start with a short paid audit (one to two weeks). You get a written report with clear priorities, estimated costs and risks. After that you decide whether to continue with us, do it in-house, or take it elsewhere. The report is yours either way.

Consulting questions

What is a fractional CTO?

A senior technology leader who works with your company part-time, typically a few hours to a couple of days a week, making technology decisions, managing vendors and engineers, and reporting to you like a full-time CTO would, at a fraction of the cost.

Is consulting only for tech companies?

No. Most of our consulting clients are non-tech businesses (manufacturing, retail, healthcare, education, professional services) who need someone on their side when dealing with software vendors, or a plan for going digital.

How is consulting priced?

Audits and roadmaps are fixed-price. Fractional CTO and advisory work is a monthly retainer. We tell you the price up front.

Get a second opinion before you sign anything.

Send us the proposal, the problem or the plan. We’ll tell you what we really think.
