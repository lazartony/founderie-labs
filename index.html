How you pay

Cash, royalty or equity. Your call.

We are the rare engineering firm that will bet on your idea alongside you. Pick the model that fits your stage.

Cash

Fixed price or monthly retainer

You pay in money — a fixed quote for a defined scope, or a monthly rate for ongoing work. Simple, predictable, no strings.

  • Fixed-scope quotes with milestone payments
  • Time & materials for evolving work
  • Monthly retainers for continuous engineering
Best for: established businesses, funded startups, agencies.
Most popular

Royalty

Lower upfront, share of revenue

We cut the upfront cost substantially in exchange for a percentage of the revenue the software generates, for an agreed period.

  • Reduced build fee (typically 30–60% less)
  • Royalty on revenue — capped or time-limited
  • Clear, written terms before we start
Best for: products with a clear revenue model and a founder who wants to keep control.

Equity / Stake

We build, we co-own

For the right idea and the right founder, we take a stake in the company instead of (or alongside) fees. We become your technical co-founder — selective by design.

  • Equity for a full product build
  • Ongoing CTO-level involvement
  • Hybrid: small fee + smaller stake
Best for: strong founders with domain expertise, traction or capital, and no technical partner.

Straight answers

Questions people ask before hiring us.

What does Founderie Labs actually do?

Three things, one company. We are a product studio that designs, builds and launches our own software products; a services team that builds websites, custom software and mobile apps for clients; and an IT consultancy that advises on technology decisions, trains teams and supplies dedicated engineers. You describe the outcome you need — we design, build, launch and maintain the technology.

I’m not technical. Will I understand what’s going on?

Yes — that is the job. You get one named point of contact who explains everything in plain business terms, a written scope you can actually read, and a working demo every week. You never need to know what a database is to know your project is on track.

How much does a project cost?

Small websites start in the low thousands of US dollars (or the equivalent in INR); custom software and apps typically range from a few thousand to tens of thousands depending on scope. After a free 30-minute call we send a written estimate with options. We also offer royalty and equity models that reduce or replace the upfront cost.

What are royalty and equity models?

Instead of paying the full fee in cash, you can pay a reduced fee plus a percentage of the revenue the software earns (royalty), or give Founderie Labs a stake in your company (equity). We accept these selectively, for ideas and founders we believe in, with clear written terms.

Where are you based and who do you work with?

Founderie Labs LLP is registered in India and works remotely with clients in India, the United States, the United Kingdom, Europe, the Middle East, Singapore and Australia. We overlap working hours with your time zone and communicate over email, WhatsApp, video calls or whatever you already use.

Do I own the code?

Yes. All code, designs, accounts and data are in your name and handed over with documentation. You are never locked in to us.

How fast can you start?

Usually within one to two weeks of agreeing the scope. Urgent fixes and consulting can start within days.

Do you have a portfolio?

Founderie Labs is a young company founded by experienced engineers, so we don’t yet publish client case studies. What we can show you is our own work: our products (starting with Kadha) are built by the same team, to the same standard. Beyond that we back our work with guarantees: written scope and price, weekly demos, full ownership, and a 30-day post-launch defect warranty. Ask us for a technical conversation — it tells you more than a logo wall would.

Tell us what you need built.

We reply within one business day with straight answers and a realistic estimate — questions first, no sales pitch.
