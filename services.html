Service questions

Can you take over software another company built?

Yes. We start with a paid technical audit (usually 1–2 weeks), tell you honestly whether it is worth keeping, and then either maintain it or plan a replacement.

Which technologies do you use?

Whatever fits the job and can be maintained for years: typically TypeScript/React/Next.js for web, Flutter or React Native for mobile, Node.js, Python or Go for backends, PostgreSQL, and AWS or Google Cloud. We avoid exotic tools that lock you in.

Do you do design too?

Yes. User-experience and interface design are part of every build. You will see and approve clickable designs before development starts.

Can you work with my existing IT vendor or team?

Absolutely. We regularly work alongside in-house teams and other vendors, and we document everything so hand-offs are clean.

Not sure which service you need?

Describe the problem. We’ll tell you the simplest thing that solves it, even if it’s not us.
