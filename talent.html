Talent & staff augmentation

Dedicated Engineers

Vetted developers, designers and DevOps engineers who join your team month-to-month, managed by us and working for you. Arranged on request, subject to availability: tell us the role and we’ll tell you honestly what we can staff.

Developers

Front-end, back-end, full-stack and mobile engineers with 3–10+ years of experience. Modern stacks: TypeScript, React, Next.js, Node, Python, Go, Flutter, React Native.

DevOps & cloud engineers

AWS, GCP, Azure, Kubernetes, CI/CD, monitoring, cost optimisation and security hardening.

Designers & QA

UX/UI designers and QA engineers (manual and automated) to complete your product team.

How it works

Tell us the role. We find the fit.

  1. Tell us the role

    Skills, seniority, hours overlap, duration.

  2. Meet 2–3 shortlisted engineers

    Already vetted by us: technical interview, code test, reference checks, communication.

  3. Start

    Onboarded into your tools and processes. A Founderie lead checks in weekly to keep quality high.

  4. Scale or stop monthly

    Add people, swap skills, or wind down with 30 days’ notice.

Talent questions

How is this different from a freelancer or a recruitment agency?

Engineers are sourced, vetted and managed by Founderie Labs: we handle vetting, payroll, equipment, code quality and replacement if someone isn’t working out. You get the flexibility of a contractor with the accountability of a company.

What is the minimum engagement?

One engineer for one month. Most clients keep engineers for six months or longer.

Can the engineer work in my time zone?

We guarantee at least four hours of overlap with your business hours, and full overlap for India, the Middle East and Europe.

Can I hire the engineer permanently later?

Yes, after a minimum period, with a simple transfer fee. We would rather you grow than feel trapped.

Need engineers?

Send us the role. We’ll tell you what we can staff, and when. If we can’t, we’ll say so.
