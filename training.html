Training

IT Training

Practical, project-based technology training — for companies that need capable teams and for individuals who want real skills.

Corporate training

Custom programmes delivered to your team, on-site or online.

  • Software & tools adoption (CRM, ERP, productivity, AI tools)
  • Cyber-security awareness for staff
  • Developer upskilling: modern web, cloud, DevOps, testing, AI-assisted engineering
  • Leadership: managing software projects and vendors

Career programmes for individuals

Small cohorts, experienced instructors, real projects.

  • Full-stack web development (12–16 weeks)
  • Mobile app development
  • Cloud & DevOps foundations
  • Applied AI for developers

Workshops & bootcamps

One- to five-day intensives on a single topic — e.g. “Build an AI assistant for your business”, “Ship a website in a weekend”, “Git and code review done right”.

Internship-to-hire pipeline

We train and evaluate fresh graduates on real work, then place the best with you — pre-vetted, productive from week one.

How we teach

Engineers teaching, not trainers reading slides.

Every instructor is a working engineer from our delivery team. Every module ends with something built and shipped. Cohorts are small enough that nobody hides.

  • Curriculum built from real client work, updated continuously
  • Live code reviews and pair programming
  • Assessment by shipped projects, not multiple-choice tests
  • Recordings, materials and a private community after the course

Training questions

Is training online or in person?

Both. Corporate programmes can be delivered on-site in India or live online for teams anywhere; individual programmes are live online with recordings.

Do you offer certificates?

Yes, a Founderie Labs certificate of completion — but we are upfront that the real value is the portfolio of working projects you build during the programme.

Can you customise a course for our company?

That is the default. We start by understanding your team’s actual work and build the curriculum around it.

Do you help trainees find jobs?

Top-performing trainees are considered for our own engineering team and for placement with clients through our talent service.

Tell us who needs training in what.

We’ll propose a programme, a schedule and a price within two business days.
